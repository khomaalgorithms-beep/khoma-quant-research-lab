Research limitations

  • Results are historical simulations. They are not predictions and do not guarantee future returns.
  • Execution is simplified: trades fill at the daily close plus a fixed cost in basis points. Real fills, spreads and market impact can be worse.
  • Taxes, borrowing costs, and fund expense ratios beyond what is reflected in adjusted prices are not modeled.
  • Yahoo Finance data is free and unofficial; it can contain errors, revisions and gaps.
  • The tested assets were chosen with hindsight (selection and survivorship bias). Assets that failed or were delisted are not in the test.
  • Strategy parameters are common defaults, but any parameter seen to work on past data may be overfitted. Check the out-of-sample (test) period before trusting a result.
